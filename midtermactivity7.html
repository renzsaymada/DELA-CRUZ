About El Nido

El Nido is a coastal town in northern Palawan, the gateway to Bacuit Bay and its 45 islands and islets. It is famous for island hopping, kayaking, snorkeling and sunsets.

Gallery

Activities

Island Hopping

Tours A, B, C and D visit lagoons, beaches and snorkeling spots, with a grilled seafood lunch on the beach.

Kayaking

Paddle through the Big and Small Lagoons, or rent a kayak at Corong-Corong for sunset.

Snorkeling

Colorful reefs at Shimizu Island, Helicopter Island and Seven Commandos Beach.

Scuba Diving

Coral walls, caves and wrecks, with discovery dives for beginners.

Hiking & Climbing

Climb Taraw Cliff or trek to Nagkalit-kalit Falls.

Sunset Cruises

Relaxed boat rides at golden hour across Bacuit Bay.

Food & Culture

Local Food

Kinilaw

Fresh raw fish cured in vinegar and calamansi, the Filipino cousin of ceviche.

Grilled Seafood

Fish, squid and prawns grilled over charcoal, served at beachfront restaurants.

Chicken Inasal

Marinated, grilled chicken served with garlic rice.

Palawan Cashews & Honey

Popular local treats and souvenirs to take home.

Culture & Traditions

El Nido is home to the Cuyonon people and indigenous communities who keep traditions of fishing, weaving and folk music alive. Town fiestas bring street dancing, music and shared feasts. Visitors are welcome to join, and a friendly "po" and "opo" go a long way when speaking with elders.

Travel Guide

Getting There

Fly to El Nido (Lio Airport) or to Puerto Princesa, then take a 5 to 6 hour van ride north.

Getting Around

Tricycles in town, motorbike rentals for beaches, and bangka boats for island tours.

Best Time to Visit

December to May has the calmest seas and sunniest weather. Book early for Holy Week.

Tips

Bring cash, reef-safe sunscreen, a dry bag and water shoes. An environmental fee applies; confirm the current amount locally.

Safety

Wear life vests on boats, follow your crew's instructions and check the weather before tours.

Contact

El Nido Municipal Tourism Office, Poblacion, El Nido, Palawan.
